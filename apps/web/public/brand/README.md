# Brand assets

The owner supplied clan-logo.png on 11 October 2026 as the new default clan logo. It is copied unchanged. The public favicon.png/favicon.ico are generated from that image with nearest-neighbour resizing and transparent padding. Administrator uploads can replace this default; GIF and animated WebP uploads retain their animation.

skull.png was supplied by the owner on 7 October 2026. Jagex attribution remains on the website.

ko-fi-blue.png is the official blue Support me on Ko-fi button, converted losslessly from the [Ko-fi creator kit](https://more.ko-fi.com/brand-assets). Source: [official blue button](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5c14e387dab576fe667689cf/670f5a01c01ea9191809398c_support_me_on_kofi_blue.avif), retrieved 11 October 2026. The button is used intact and links to the owner's Ko-fi page.
